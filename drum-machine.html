How the grid works

Each row is a drum. Each column is a 16th-note step in a one-bar loop. Count it as 1 e & a 2 e & a 3 e & a 4 e & a.

Quick groove map

Kick gives weight, usually on 1 and sometimes 3.
Snare often lands on beats 2 and 4.
Hi-hat keeps time with steady repeated notes.
